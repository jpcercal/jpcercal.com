PHPSC Conf 2010

Engineering João Paulo Cercal The coordination team of the Santa Catarina State PHP Users Group – PHPSC – is holding PHPSC Conf 2010 in Joinville/SC in partnership with SOCIESC. https://staging.jpcercal.com/pr-148/en/phpsc-conf-2010/index.svg https://staging.jpcercal.com/pr-148/en/phpsc-conf-2010/index.dark.svg en post

The coordination team of the Santa Catarina State PHP Users Group – PHPSC – is holding the third edition of the event in Joinville/SC, in partnership with SOCIESC, during its computing programs’ academic week, on August 27 and 28, 2010, entitled: PHPSC Conf 2010.

The suggested topics cover the following areas:

  • PHP (version 5.3 and future versions, frameworks, development best practices, IDEs, integration with other languages, mobile);
  • Case studies of companies using PHP as a technological solution;
  • Agile development (scrum, XP and other related methodologies);
  • RIA (Javascript frameworks, Flex, Silverlight), CSS, HTML5;
  • How to contribute to the community and how to help fix and improve PHP;

Come join us at the event PHPSC Conf 2010.
