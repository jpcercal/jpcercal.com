Responsive Sites gain points in Google's rankings

Business Elisete dos Santos Cercal Want to appear among Google's first search results? On mobile devices, research shows that 61% of visitors to a non-responsive page return to Google’s search page looking for a site that lets them view the content better.

Knowing this, the responsive web design market has been consolidating and becoming unified. Because keeping all the elements of the page’s desktop version viewable on other devices has already gone beyond an advantage and become a requirement. Mobile devices are gaining strength every day, in 2012 for every five computers sold one tablet was sold, compared with 2011, when for every fourteen computers one tablet was sold.

Being on Google’s first pages of search results is what everyone who has a website wants, seeking to show the best search content, Google gives priority to responsive sites, because they follow the rule that the more visits, the better your position in search rankings. Previously, sites were developed differently for each device, such as personal computers and cell phones. So the visits your site received through a personal computer weren’t the same as visits made to the site with a cell phone. However, today with a responsive website your visits have become unified, thus increasing your level in Google’s search rankings.
