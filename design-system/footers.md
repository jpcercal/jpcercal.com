All three share the same links (home, search, contact, authors, categories,
tags, RSS, source, the author's social profiles) and the same theme tokens.
The one rendered on every real page is picked with `params.footerVariant`
in `config.yaml` (`compact`, `columns` or `centered`).

1. **Compact**: one quiet line, copyright left and navigation right.
2. **Columns**: tinted band with the site description, Explore and Connect columns and a source bar.
3. **Centered**: centered brand and tagline, social profiles as outlined buttons (same border, radius and height as the header controls) and a navigation line.
