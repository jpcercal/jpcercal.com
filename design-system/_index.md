This section documents the visual language of the blog. It is **staging-only**:
the `preview-staging` job copies `design-system/` into `content/` with a
`--buildDrafts` build, while production builds never see it.

- [Tokens](tokens/): colors, fonts, spacing.
- [Components](components/): rendered examples of the building blocks.
