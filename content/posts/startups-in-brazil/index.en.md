The dream of Brazil's IT visionaries is closer to becoming reality, on April 15 a
_StartUp_ program created by the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (MCTI) was launched by the government, the call
for applications from interested _startups_ is on the
Start-Up Brasil program's website (2013 call for applications, now offline).

Around 30% of the competing _startups_ will be international, applications open on April 25 and close
on May 31, the results of this first stage will be released from June 25 on the _CNPq_ website and in the
Official Gazette of the Union.

The program will also offer another opportunity for those who don't pass the first stage, the
same rules from the first stage's call for applications will be used, and it will begin on November 19 with results released
from December 16.

![Startups in Brazil](jobs-act-what-crowdfunding-means-for-your-startup-533851e64c-300x168.jpg "Startups in Brazil")

To carry out this project the government selected some companies already established in the market to act as
accelerators for the _startups_, there will be nine in total, but those announced are _Aceleratech_, _Microsoft_, _Papaya_, _Pipa_
and _Wayra_, these companies will act as incubators and should support eight to ten _startups_.

The government's goal is to accelerate 150 _startups_ by 2014, for this it anticipates financial support of R$ 40 million,
offering R$ 200 thousand to each _startup_, it also expects at least 40 _startups_ to benefit from this project,
which is part of the Strategic Software and Information Technology Services Program (TI Maior).
