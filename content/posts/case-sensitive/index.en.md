_"Case sensitive"_ is a term indicating that text is sensitive to letter case, meaning it distinguishes between
uppercase and lowercase letters.

We'll see how it works in practice by looking at the situations below:

**Case Sensitive**

_texto_ is different from _Texto_, because the second word starts with a capitalized consonant.

**Case Insensitive**

_texto_ is the same as _Texto_, there is no distinction between letter cases.

A related concept joins words and capitalizes the letters at the beginning of each word,
called [CamelCase]({{< ref "posts/the-camelcase-convention/index.md" >}} "The CamelCase Convention").
