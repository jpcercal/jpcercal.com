_Android_, _Google's_ mobile operating system, accounted for 79% of digital threats created for mobile devices
in 2012. A year earlier, that figure was 66%, and in 2010, 11%. The data was released this Thursday (7) by
_F-Secure_, a Finnish security software company. The company identified 301 new threats and variants targeting
_smartphones_ and _tablets_ exclusively during the year.

![Android has 79% of threats to mobile devices](index.jpg "Android has 79% of threats to mobile devices")

_Apple's iOS_, the second most popular mobile operating system among _smartphones_, reached just 0.7% of
viruses created for mobile devices. The _F-Secure_ survey also highlighted the decline in threats developed for
_Nokia's Symbian_. For a long time, devices based on this system dominated sales in the mobile phone
market. According to the study, _Symbian_ was targeted by 19% of threats to mobile devices in 2012, compared with 29%
in 2011 and 62% in 2010.

In the fourth quarter, a large share of threats created for _Android_ and detected by _F-Secure_ involved malicious
programs that install themselves, through various means, on users' devices. These programs then automatically send
text messages (_SMS_) to numbers registered by cybercriminals in certain countries.

Similar to the old 0900 and known as _premium_ numbers, these services are charged to the customer and the revenues
are passed on to hackers by operators, who are unaware of or lack the means to identify and curb this practice.
This type of attack is one of the most common on mobile devices. Of the 96 _Android_ threat variants detected
by _F-Secure_ between October and December, 21 followed this approach.

Hackers' broad interest in developing specific threats for _Android_ follows a natural logic of gaining
scale: concentrating efforts on the systems with the greatest consumer appeal. According to data from research company
_Gartner_, _Android_-based devices reached a 69.7% share of total _smartphone_ sales
in the fourth quarter. In the same period of 2011, that figure was 51.3%.
