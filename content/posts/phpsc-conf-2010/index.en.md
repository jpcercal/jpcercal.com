The coordination team of the **Santa Catarina State PHP Users Group** – PHPSC – is holding the third edition of
the event in **Joinville/SC**, in partnership with SOCIESC, during its computing programs' academic week, on
**August 27 and 28, 2010**, entitled: **PHPSC Conf 2010**.

The
**suggested topics** cover the following areas:

* PHP (version 5.3 and future versions, frameworks, development best practices, IDEs, integration with other
languages, mobile);
* Case studies of companies using PHP as a technological solution;
* Agile development (scrum, XP and other related methodologies);
* RIA (Javascript frameworks, Flex, Silverlight), CSS, HTML5;
* How to contribute to the community and how to help fix and improve PHP;

> Come join us at the event [PHPSC Conf 2010](http://www.phpsc.com.br/).
