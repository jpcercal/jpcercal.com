That's right, Android began with a focus on cameras, according to Andy Rubin, creator of the original
system, the initial intention of his invention was to reach the photography market.

However, Google acquired Android Inc in 2005 and the plans gradually changed and became more
ambitious, making the platform the success it is today.

Recently Android reached 1.5 million activations per day, to think that the story could have been
different, Android an operating system for digital cameras.

![Android was software for cameras](index.jpeg "Android was software for cameras")

"In the end, we decided that the digital camera market wasn't big enough. Still, I was worried about
Symbian and Microsoft. The iPhone didn't even exist", said Rubin, who imagined Android as a perfect connection between
the PC and the camera.

After selling his company to Google, Rubin worked as the leader of the Android operating system within
Google for 8 years, last month he left his position to work in other areas with the idea of starting
a new chapter of creations.
