The viral hit of the moment has already reached 1 billion views in 40 days, the number of views wasn't achieved with just
one posted video, but through scenes from other videos, according to the company
_Visible Measures_,
just the ten most popular versions of "_Harlem Shake_" account for a total of 336 million views.

![The viral Harlem Shake](nasa-harlem-shake-300x163.jpg "The viral Harlem Shake")

However, this viral hit ended up causing some trouble, for example, at a mining company in Australia 15
miners were fired after posting a video of the dance during working hours and at their workplace. Here in Brazil, in Goiânia,
a school suspended students who made the _Harlem Shake_ video in the bathroom. However, some companies joined the
trend and got everyone dancing, among them NASA and Facebook.

Now we just have to wait and see whether _Harlem Shake_ will surpass the _Gangnam Style_ mark.
