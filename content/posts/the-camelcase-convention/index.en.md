**CamelCase** (_camel case_) is the practice of writing compound words or phrases with their elements joined,
without spaces, and the first letter of each element capitalized, meaning the uppercase letters "_jump_" out among the
lowercase ones like a camel's humps.

![The CamelCase Convention](camelcase.jpg "The CamelCase Convention")

Example: "_meuComputador_, "_jogoDeFutebol_" or "_linguagemProgramacao_".
