The Pirate Bay leads its segment

  • Portuguese: The Pirate Bay é líder em seu segmento

In 2011 the Torret Freak website conducted a survey of file-sharing sites that showed the cyberlocker model dominating the ‘market’. 4shared was the leader, while The Pirate Bay occupied only sixth place.

Today the story is different, Pirate Bay has taken first place, according to Torrent Freak’s analysis, the title was acquired through the declining popularity of hosting sites such as 4Shared and Megaupload, after Megaupload’s closure and anti-piracy measures these sites saw a drop in usage. Making Pirate Bay the world’s most famous and most used site for downloading files.

Thus, files for sharing are based on BitTorrent on half of the top ten sites, compared with just two in 2011, RapidShare and FileServe. According to Torrent Freak, the figures include only English-language file-sharing sites and were calculated by comparing traffic and various analytical tools, such as Compete, Quantcast and Alexa.
