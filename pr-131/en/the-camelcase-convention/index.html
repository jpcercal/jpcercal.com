The CamelCase Convention

Engineering João Paulo Cercal CamelCase is the practice of writing compound words with their elements joined, without spaces, and the first letter of each element capitalized. https://staging.jpcercal.com/pr-131/en/the-camelcase-convention/index.svg https://staging.jpcercal.com/pr-131/en/the-camelcase-convention/index.dark.svg en post

CamelCase (camel case) is the practice of writing compound words or phrases with their elements joined, without spaces, and the first letter of each element capitalized, meaning the uppercase letters “jump” out among the lowercase ones like a camel’s humps.

Example: “meuComputador, “jogoDeFutebol” or “linguagemProgramacao”.
